Frequently asked questions

What should I do if my car overheats?

Pull over safely, turn off the engine immediately, and allow it to cool before checking levels. Never open a hot radiator cap.

How often should coolant be flushed?

Most auto manufacturers recommend a cooling system flush every 30,000–60,000 miles.
