Maintenance Built Specifically for Electric Vehicles

While electric vehicles eliminate engine oil changes and spark plugs, they feature heavy battery packs, high torque loads, and unique wear patterns that demand specialized care:

  • EV Brake Maintenance & Fluid Flushes: Because regenerative braking handles most deceleration, physical brake calipers often rust or stick due to non-use. We clean, lube, and service brake sliders, and replace high-moisture brake fluid.
  • Suspension & Alignment Service: EV battery weight places significant strain on control arms, ball joints, bushings, and dampeners. We replace worn suspension units and realign tires to prevent uneven tread wear.
  • Cabin Air Filters & HEPA Service: Replacing dirty cabin filters and desiccant bags to maintain clean interior cabin climate and defrost performance.
  • Tire Replacement & Rotation: Installing EV-specific quiet tires designed to carry high vehicle curb weight and handle instant electric torque.

High-Voltage Safety Protocols

Working on electric powertrains requires certified training and specialized insulated tools rated for high-voltage DC current. Whether handling 12V auxiliary battery replacements, cooling loop flushes, or suspension upgrades, our team follows strict factory safety guidelines to safeguard your vehicle's battery pack and electronic architecture.

Frequently asked questions

Why do Tesla tires wear out faster than gas car tires?

Electric vehicles carry heavy lithium-ion battery packs and produce instant electric torque from a dead stop. This extra weight and immediate acceleration place extra friction on tire treads, requiring frequent rotations.

Do electric cars really need brake service if they use regenerative braking?

Yes! Because friction brakes are used less frequently due to regenerative braking, caliper pins and pads can accumulate rust, road grit, and moisture, causing them to seize up if not regularly cleaned and lubricated.

Will servicing my Tesla at an independent shop void my factory warranty?

No. Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, routine maintenance and repairs performed by a qualified independent shop will not void your manufacturer warranty.
