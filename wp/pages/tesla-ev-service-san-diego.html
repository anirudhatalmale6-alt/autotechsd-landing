Frequently asked questions

Do Tesla brakes wear out?

Yes, although regenerative braking reduces wear, physical pads and slide pins still require periodic inspection.

Can you service a Tesla's 12-volt battery?

Yes — the 12V auxiliary battery is separate from the main traction pack, and we test and replace it on site.
