Auto Windshield Replacement & Repair

Repair it early, replace it when it matters

Small chips and cracks can often be repaired in under an hour if caught early, saving you the cost of a full replacement. Once a crack spreads beyond a few inches or sits in the driver's direct line of sight, replacement is the safer option.

Many newer vehicles mount a camera or sensor to the windshield for lane-departure and collision-avoidance systems. After a replacement, that system needs to be recalibrated so it reads the road correctly — we handle that as part of the service.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can a small windshield chip be repaired instead of replaced?

Often, yes — chips smaller than a quarter and not in the driver's direct line of sight can usually be repaired rather than replaced, especially if addressed quickly.

Does my new windshield need to be recalibrated?

If your vehicle has a forward-facing camera for lane-departure or collision-avoidance systems, yes — recalibration ensures those systems read the road correctly after replacement.

Does insurance cover windshield replacement?

Many comprehensive auto insurance policies cover windshield replacement, sometimes with no deductible. We can work directly with most insurers.
