Fast Rock Chip Repair vs. Replacement

If a flying stone creates a small bullseye or star chip on your glass, immediate repair can stop the crack from spreading across the entire windshield.

  • Rock Chip Repair: We inject high-grade optical resin into small chips (smaller than a quarter) to restore structural integrity and optical clarity in under 30 minutes.
  • Full Replacement: Recommended if cracks extend beyond 3 inches, cross directly in front of the driver's line of sight, or reach the outer edge of the glass perimeter.

ADAS Camera Calibration Included

Modern vehicles equipped with safety features like Lane Keep Assist, Automatic Emergency Braking, and Adaptive Cruise Control rely on cameras mounted directly behind the front windshield. Whenever glass is replaced, these cameras must be recalibrated to OEM specifications. We handle precision ADAS recalibration in-house so your driver assistance features operate safely.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can a chipped windshield be repaired, or does it always need replacement?

If the chip or crack is smaller than a credit card and not directly in the driver's primary line of vision or on the outer edge of the glass, it can usually be repaired safely without replacing the entire windshield.

What is ADAS windshield calibration?

ADAS calibration adjusts the alignment of safety cameras mounted on your windshield. Even a fraction-of-a-millimeter shift during glass installation can misalign lane-departure or emergency-braking systems if not calibrated.

How long after windshield replacement can I drive my vehicle?

Thanks to high-grade, fast-curing adhesives, most vehicles are safe to drive within 1 to 2 hours after installation is completed.
