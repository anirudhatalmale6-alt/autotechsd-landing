Comprehensive Battery Diagnostics & Load Testing

Not every starting issue is caused by a failed battery. Alternator failures, corroded battery terminals, starter motor drag, or parasitic electrical drains can mimic the exact symptoms of a dead battery. Before installing a new unit, our team performs a complete digital load test and charging system inspection. We analyze your battery's Cold Cranking Amps (CCA), check the alternator's voltage output, and clean terminal connectors to ensure optimal performance across your vehicle's entire electrical network.

Warning signs

Signs You Need Your Battery Tested

Any of these means it is worth getting checked before it becomes a bigger repair.

  • Slow Engine Crank: The engine turns over sluggishly or takes longer than usual to start.
  • Clicking Sound: A rapid clicking noise when turning the ignition key or pressing the start button.
  • Dim Headlights & Cabin Lights: Electronics flicker or dim when idling or starting the vehicle.
  • Dashboard Warning Light: The battery or charging system indicator remains illuminated.
  • Swollen Battery Case: Heat exposure causing the battery housing to swell or bulge.

Frequently asked questions

How long do car batteries usually last in San Diego?

Car batteries in San Diego typically last between 3 to 5 years. While our climate avoids extreme freezing temperatures, summer heat and coastal humidity can accelerate internal grid corrosion and liquid evaporation inside flooded batteries.

Do modern cars need battery registration after replacement?

Yes, many modern European and late-model vehicles require battery registration or resetting the Battery Management System (BMS). This tells the car's computer that a new battery is installed so it adjusts charging rates properly.
