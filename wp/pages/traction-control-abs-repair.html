How ABS & Traction Control Protect You

  • Anti-Lock Braking System (ABS): Prevents your wheels from locking up during emergency braking, allowing you to maintain steering control.
  • Traction Control System (TCS): Detects wheel spin during acceleration and automatically reduces engine power or applies selective braking to individual wheels to restore grip.
  • Electronic Stability Control (ESC): Monitors steering angle and vehicle trajectory, intervening to prevent skidding or fishtailing during sudden emergency maneuvers.

Diagnostic Capabilities

When an ABS or TCS light turns on, the system usually deactivates itself entirely as a safety precaution, reverting your car to conventional braking without anti-lock protection. Our technicians hook into your vehicle's computer systems to analyze sensor wheel speeds, measure solenoid valve resistance, test module communication lines, and replace damaged wheel speed sensor harnesses.

Common Failure Points

  • Wheel Speed Sensors: Mounted near the wheel hubs, these sensors are constantly exposed to road debris, water, and heat wear.
  • ABS Hydraulic Pump/Module: Internal electrical valve failures or pump motor wear.
  • Tone Rings: Cracked or rusted reluctor rings preventing clean magnetic readings.
  • Brake Pedal Position Sensors: Faulty signals sent to the engine control module.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to drive with the ABS light on?

Your regular hydraulic brakes will still stop the vehicle, but the anti-lock safety features and traction control will not function during an emergency hard stop. You should have the system inspected as soon as possible.

Why did my ABS and Traction Control lights come on at the same time?

These systems share the exact same wheel speed sensors and computer control modules. A single faulty wheel sensor or wheel bearing issue often triggers both warning lights simultaneously.

How much does it cost to replace a wheel speed sensor in San Diego?

Costs vary depending on vehicle make and accessibility, but replacing a wheel speed sensor is a straightforward, budget-friendly repair compared to replacing full hydraulic modules.
