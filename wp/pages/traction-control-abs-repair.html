Frequently asked questions

What does the traction control light mean?

It indicates a wheel-speed sensor fault, an electronic module error, or that the system engaged to prevent wheel slip.

Is it safe to drive with the ABS light on?

Your basic mechanical brakes still work, but anti-lock emergency assistance is disabled — which increases stopping distance on slippery roads.
