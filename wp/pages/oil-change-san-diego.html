Why Quality Engine Oil Matters

Engine oil cleans internal components, reduces extreme friction between moving parts, cools engine heat, and seals internal clearances. Over time, heat breakdown and engine contaminants turn fresh oil into dark sludgy deposits. Operating your vehicle on degraded oil increases internal engine friction, degrades gas mileage, and eventually risks catastrophic internal engine failure.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How often should I change my oil?

For full synthetic oil, change intervals are typically every 5,000 to 7,500 miles. Conventional oil generally requires changing every 3,000 to 5,000 miles. Always consult your owner's manual or ask our technicians for recommendations based on your driving conditions.

Can I switch from conventional oil to full synthetic?

Yes, switching to synthetic oil is safe for almost all modern engines and provides significantly better protection against thermal breakdown, engine sludging, and internal wear.

Why does my car burn oil between changes?

Minor oil consumption can occur naturally, especially in modern engines with low-friction piston rings or high-mileage vehicles with worn valve seals. However, noticeable drops in oil level should be inspected for leaks or internal component wear.
