Frequently asked questions

How often should transmission fluid be changed?

Most auto manufacturers recommend replacing transmission fluid every 30,000 to 60,000 miles to prevent fluid breakdown and internal friction.

What are the symptoms of a slipping transmission?

Slipping manifests as RPM spikes without acceleration, delayed gear engagement, erratic shifting, or unusual humming noises while driving.
