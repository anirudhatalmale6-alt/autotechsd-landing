Silver Package — $150

  • Hand Exterior Wash: Multi-bucket gentle foam wash to prevent swirl marks.
  • Wheel & Tire Package: Cleaning of rims, removal of surface brake dust, and satin tire shine application.
  • Interior Vacuum & Wipe-Down: Thorough vacuuming of footwells, mats, seats, and trunk area, followed by a console wipe down.

Gold Package — $250

Our signature total-vehicle restoration package. Designed for deep stain removal, complete paint decontamination, and multi-month ceramic protection.

  • Includes Everything in the Silver Package PLUS:
  • Clay Bar Paint Decontamination: Removes embedded rail dust, sap, and industrial fallout, leaving paint glass-smooth.
  • Carpet & Upholstery Shampoo: Hot water extraction to lift stubborn spills, dirt, ground-in mud, and odors.
  • Leather Deep Clean & Conditioning: Nourishes leather seats to prevent cracking and restore soft natural texture.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does auto detailing cost at Auto Tech Specialists?

We offer two simple, upfront price tiers: our Silver Package for $150 and our Gold Package for $250. Pricing is fixed for most passenger sedans, coupes, and light crossovers. Larger trucks or oversized 3-row SUVs may incur a slight size adjustment.

What is the difference between Silver and Gold detailing?

The Silver Package ($150) covers routine interior vacuuming, exterior hand washing, glass cleaning, and light spray wax. The Gold Package ($250) adds paint clay bar treatment, carpet shampooing, hot steam sanitation, leather conditioning, and durable ceramic paint sealant.

How long does a detailing appointment take?

A Silver detail takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours. The Gold detail requires 3 to 4 hours to allow full drying times for carpet extraction and sealant curing.

Do I need an appointment for auto detailing?

While we accept walk-in clients at our 7950 Clairemont Mesa Blvd location, reserving your time slot in advance guarantees prompt service in our dedicated detail bay.
