Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between the collision repair page and the body shop page?

Collision repair is typically insurance-claim work after an accident. Auto body shop services cover everyday cosmetic repairs — dents, scratches, and paint — whether or not it's insurance-related.

Can a dent be fixed without repainting?

Many minor dents without paint damage can be fixed with paintless dent repair, which preserves the factory paint and costs less than traditional bodywork.

How do you match paint color exactly?

We pull your vehicle's factory paint code and mix to match, so the repaired panel blends seamlessly with the rest of the vehicle.
