Frequently asked questions

How long does auto body paint repair take?

Minor bumper repairs or paint touch-ups typically take 2 to 3 days, while major collision repairs requiring panel replacements and multi-stage paint drying can take 1 to 2 weeks.

What is Paintless Dent Repair (PDR)?

PDR is a specialized technique that uses custom metal tools to gently push dents out from the back of a body panel. It requires no body filler or repainting, preserving your vehicle's original factory finish.

Do I have to use the body shop my insurance company recommends?

No. California law guarantees you the right to have your vehicle repaired at any licensed auto body shop you choose. Insurance companies cannot force you to use their preferred drive-in facilities.
