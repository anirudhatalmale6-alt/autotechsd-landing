footer creates all 11 links at once, already linked and already spaced and sized

STEPS
1.  WordPress admin > Appearance > Widgets
2.  Find the box headed SERVICES. It is the column that currently reads:
    Smog Check & Inspection / Auto Body & Collision Repair / Check Engine Light /
    Emergency Repair / Tune-Up & Maintenance / Contact Us
3.  Click at the very end of "Tune-Up & Maintenance", after the last letter.
4.  Press the End key, then press Enter. That gives you an empty line under it.
5.  Select everything below the dashed line here - from the first <!-- down to
    the very last --> - copy it, and paste onto that empty line.
    WordPress reads the code and turns it into 11 finished links.
6.  Click Update, top right. Then load the site and scroll to the footer.
